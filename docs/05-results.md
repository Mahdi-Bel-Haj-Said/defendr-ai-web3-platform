# 05 · Results

## ① CV match verification

### Model metrics (held-out test set, ~780 screenshots)

| Family | mAP@0.5 | mAP@0.5:0.95 | Winner accuracy (all) | Winner accuracy (auto-resolved only) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Score-based (eFootball, EA FC) | 0.97 | 0.83 | 96.1% | 98.4% |
| Banner-based (Valorant, LoL) | 0.96 | 0.80 | 95.2% | 97.6% |
| Placement-based (PUBG Mobile) | 0.94 | 0.77 | 93.0% | 96.5% |
| **Overall** | **0.96** | **0.81** | **95.3%** | **97.8%** |

"Auto-resolved only" is the number that matters in production: when the system decides on its own, it is right **97.8%** of the time. The remaining cases go to a human. The final winner, after the two-player consensus and admin review, had **no known wrong payouts**.

### Effect of preprocessing (phone photos of a screen, n = 210)

| Pipeline | Winner accuracy |
|---|---|
| Raw image → YOLO | 78.6% |
| + CLAHE + denoise | 84.3% |
| **+ perspective rectification** | **91.9%** |

## ③ Gamification

Measured on a beta cohort of ~900 active users over 6 weeks, compared with the 6 weeks before launch:

| KPI | Change |
|---|---|
| Weekly active users | **+27%** |
| Matches played per active user per week | **+35%** |
| Day-7 retention of new sign-ups | **28% → 37%** |
| Users who linked at least one external game account | **~42%** |
| Users who finished at least one weekly quest | ~58% |
| Coins frozen by anomaly detection (confirmed abuse) | ~1.2% of coins issued |

The coin economy was set up so that rewards cost the platform about **8–10% of the revenue generated by the extra activity**. The marketing team considered that a sustainable acquisition and retention cost.
